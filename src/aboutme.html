// Biography

I'm an Applied Computer Science student at Thomas More, focused on AI, machine learning, data, and full-stack development. I like clear thinking, practical execution, and building things that actually work in real life.

Before starting my CS degree, I spent about two years at Renaissance Downtown Dubai. I worked across F&B, guest services, and front office, including high-pressure periods where things changed fast. That experience taught me how to stay professional, communicate clearly, and operate within brand standards while still taking ownership when it mattered.

What guides how I work is simple: ownership, reliability, consistency, and follow-through. I'd rather commit to fewer things and do them properly than spread myself thin, and I hold myself to that with the same integrity whether anyone's watching or not.

I also enjoy intellectually stimulating conversations, and I genuinely see myself as a lifelong learner.

// Professional Ambition

My professional ambition right now is to learn as much as possible. I am focused on building strong skills through real projects and consistent hands-on practice.

I want to work in environments that challenge me to think critically and creatively, where I can contribute meaningfully to projects with a positive impact on society. I am especially interested in building software and using data to improve decision-making, streamline operations, and uncover patterns that lead to meaningful insights.
